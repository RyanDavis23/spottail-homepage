About us

Backed by twenty years of local broadcast.

Spottail Digital is a subsidiary of Cunningham Broadcasting Corporation, an independent television broadcast company with over 20 years in the industry that owns and / or operates 20 television stations in 18 markets across the United States.

That heritage is the point. Spottail was built by people who sell local advertising for a living, for the advertisers and agencies who buy it — which is why the platform optimizes for the things local buyers actually need: real geography, honest pacing, and one report at the end of it.
